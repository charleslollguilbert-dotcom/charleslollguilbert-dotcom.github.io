AI Consulting - 03

Decision advisory.

A scoped engagement on a single hard question - a platform bet, build vs. buy, a multi-source strategy, an architecture you can’t un-choose. AI-mapped, expert-validated, and delivered as a decision you can defend.

Bring a question
The engagement

Three moves, no black box.

i.

Frame

You bring the question; we structure it - stakes, constraints, time horizon, hidden assumptions. Many clients reframe the problem in this step alone.

ii.

Map & stress-test

AI maps the option space and runs the scenarios; two to three senior experts challenge it and add what only experience can see.

iii.

Decide & defend

You receive a board-ready package: recommendation, full rationale, alternatives considered, and the risk profile of each path.
