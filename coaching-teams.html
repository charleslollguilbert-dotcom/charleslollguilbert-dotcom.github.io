Executive Coaching - 03

Coaching the top team.

The CODIR or executive committee is the one team whose dysfunction is most expensive and least discussed. We coach it as a unit - individual depth plus collective alignment - so it decides faster and trusts harder.

Discuss your ExCo
Two layers

Individuals and the system between them.

/ Individual

Depth

Selective one-to-one work with members of the team, so personal blind spots stop showing up as collective friction.

/ Collective

Alignment

Facilitated sessions on trust, conflict, commitment and accountability - the patterns that decide whether good people make good decisions together.

Pairs naturally with

This often runs alongside team training.

Top-team coaching and the Lencioni / Working Genius workshops are built to reinforce each other - the workshop gives the team shared language, the coaching makes it stick. See the training pillar →
