AI Consulting - 01 - Flagship

Tool-D.

An AI-powered FPGA vendor selection platform. Set your project context and the weights that matter, and Tool-D ranks vendors live, validates the top results with named experts, and generates a board-ready decision package - so you decide on objective, labelled criteria.

Request a demo
The platform

Decide based on objective criteria.

Three parts, one flow. The AI engine pulls vendor data and computes a weighted scorecard. An expert layer annotates the leading options with real, credentialed judgement. And a single click produces the defensible artefact: a decision package your board will accept.

The moment the demo earns belief is simple - move a weight slider, and the ranking re-orders in front of you. Nothing is hidden in a black box.

How it works

From question to defensible answer in days.

i.

Context & weights

Enter the project - application, lifecycle, volume, constraints - then set sliders on the criteria that matter: performance, power, cost, supply resilience, security, second-source.

ii.

AI + expert pass

The engine computes a weighted, live-updating scorecard and ranks the vendors. Two to three named experts annotate the top results with a line of judgement and their credentials.

iii.

Decision package

One click generates a PDF: a one-page scorecard, a short rationale, a risk register and recommended next steps. Built to be presented, not just filed.
