New SaaS - EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance

crAcken - the CRA flight recorder for connected products.

The CRA reporting clock starts on 11 September 2026 - fifteen months before the deadline most teams are planning to. crAcken connects SBOMs, vulnerability findings and security decisions into one traceable release workflow, so your organisation can meet the 24h / 72h reporting windows and prove what it knew, decided, and did. Scanners find vulnerabilities; crAcken proves your response.

  • Deterministic SBOM analysis - CycloneDX / SPDX, no LLM on the critical path
  • Per-release monitoring with alerts, awareness timestamps and named owners
  • Case workflow, human-reviewed AI memo, and an audit-ready evidence bundle
